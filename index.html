Sakshi — AI-Assisted Personal Safety

My flagship project: a personal safety platform designed to detect patterns of potentially threatening behaviour using camera and microphone sensors.

I built the pipeline using YOLO, ByteTrack, pose estimation, threat scoring, Gemini verification, authentication, and incident logging. SOS escalation occurs after verification, with an option for the user to cancel.

Technologies: Python, OpenCV, YOLO, MediaPipe, FastAPI, WebSockets, Gemini, Supabase.
